# Loomipaw — Launch Marketing Plan (First 30 Days)

A disciplined 30-day launch: validate tracking, seed organic proof, then buy
traffic **only after** the funnel and pixels are proven. Sequenced so spend
follows signal.

All figures are **planning estimates for a premium pet DTC launch**, not
guarantees. Scale on measured performance, not hope.

---

## Pre-flight (before Day 1) — must be green

- [ ] Go-live checklist complete (`FINAL_GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md`): live payments,
      HTTPS, email, backups.
- [ ] Analytics installed + verified (`EVENT_TRACKING_VERIFICATION.md`):
      GA4, Meta, TikTok, Clarity all firing `purchase`/`CompletePayment`.
- [ ] Meta + TikTok pixels warmed; catalog feed uploaded (for dynamic retargeting).
- [ ] Lifecycle emails live (`EMAIL_MARKETING_STRATEGY.md`): welcome + abandoned
      cart at minimum.
- [ ] Search Console verified + sitemap submitted (`SEO_GROWTH_PLAN.md`).
- [ ] 20–30 UGC/creative assets ready (photo + video) for organic + ads.

## Week 1 — Validation & organic seeding (spend: ~$0 paid)

**Objective:** prove the funnel with real orders; build initial proof.

- **Tracking validation:** run the end-to-end smoke test daily; confirm GA4
  revenue == real orders. Do **not** start paid until `purchase` is verified.
- **Organic content:** launch IG/TikTok, post 1–2×/day — product-in-use,
  unboxing, "designed for the bond" storytelling, before/after. Reuse homepage
  UGC.
- **Warm audience:** email the existing newsletter list (10% welcome), announce
  launch to friends/family/waitlist.
- **Influencer/UGC outreach:** contact 20–40 micro dog-creators (5k–50k), offer
  free product for honest content + affiliate code. Prioritize gifting over cash.
- **KPI:** first 10–25 orders; ≥1 review per SKU; creator replies.

## Week 2 — First paid tests (spend: ~$50–100/day)

**Objective:** find 1–2 winning audience/creative combos on prospecting.

- **Meta Advantage+ / broad** + 2–3 interest sets (dog owners, luxury pet,
  sustainability). 3–5 creatives (UGC video first). Optimize for **Purchase**
  (or `AddToCart` early if volume is thin, then switch).
- **TikTok** Spark Ads on the best organic posts; optimize `CompletePayment`.
- **Structure:** 1 campaign, 3–4 ad sets, 3–5 ads each; $15–25/ad set/day.
- **Testing framework:** change **one variable at a time** (hook, format,
  audience). Kill an ad set at ≥$20 spend with $0 sales or CTR <0.8%; scale
  what beats target CPA.
- **KPI:** identify winners; **CPA ≤ 40–50% of AOV**; ROAS ≥ 1.5–2.0 (early).

## Week 3 — Retargeting (add ~$20–40/day)

**Objective:** convert the traffic Weeks 1–2 generated.

- **Warm retargeting** from pixel audiences: `ViewContent` + `AddToCart`
  non-purchasers (7–14 day), dynamic product ads from the catalog feed.
- **Abandoned-cart email** already recovering; layer ad retargeting on top.
- **Offer:** free shipping reminder or first-order 10% (already in welcome) —
  avoid discount-training the audience.
- **KPI:** retargeting ROAS ≥ 3–5 (should beat prospecting); overall blended
  ROAS trending up.

## Week 4 — Scale winners (spend: 1.5–2× prior, gated on ROAS)

**Objective:** scale profitably, expand creative.

- Increase budget on winning ad sets **20–30% every 2–3 days** (avoid learning
  resets), or duplicate into higher budgets.
- Broaden: lookalikes from purchasers, new interest sets, new placements.
- **Creative refresh:** 5–10 new UGC variants to fight fatigue (watch frequency
  >2–3).
- Push **AOV** with the live bundles / "frequently bought together" / free-ship
  threshold (see `REVENUE_GROWTH_REPORT.md`).
- **KPI:** stable/º improving blended ROAS at higher spend; repeat-purchase rate
  climbing via post-purchase flow.

## Testing framework (applies to all channels)

1. **One variable per test** (audience OR creative OR hook OR offer).
2. **Minimum data before deciding:** ~$20–30 spend or ~1,000 impressions per ad.
3. **Kill / keep / scale:** kill $0-sale losers early; keep break-even for more
   data; scale profitable winners slowly.
4. **Creative is the lever:** 70% of performance is the hook/first 3 seconds —
   test formats (UGC, testimonial, before/after, founder) not just audiences.
5. **Document every test** (hypothesis → result → decision) so learnings compound.

---

## Risk guardrails

- No paid scale until `purchase` tracking is verified across GA4 + pixels.
- Watch inventory (`Only N left` is honest scarcity — don't oversell).
- Cap discounting; protect margin and brand positioning.
- Monitor Clarity recordings weekly for new friction as traffic grows.
