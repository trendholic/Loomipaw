# Loomipaw — Customer Acquisition Plan

Segment-by-segment acquisition strategy, grounded in the real catalog (premium
dog apparel, harnesses/leads, orthopedic beds, leather collars, ID tags,
matching sets) and positioning (luxury, sustainable OEKO-TEX®, comfort-tested,
30-day guarantee, $75 free shipping).

Use with `PAID_AD_TESTING_PLAN.md` (where to spend) and
`CONTENT_CREATIVE_SYSTEM.md` (what to make). Angles are hypotheses to test, not
certainties.

---

## Segment 1 — Everyday dog owners (core)

- **Pain points:** cheap gear that wears out, poor fit, ugly products that clash
  with the home, decision fatigue.
- **Buying triggers:** worn-out harness/bed, seasonal weather, a new "hero" find
  on social, free-shipping threshold.
- **Best products:** Trailhead Adventure Harness ($64), City Rope Lead ($44),
  Aspen Merino Knit ($78), Nap Nest Bolster Bed ($148).
- **Advertising angle:** *"Gear as good as your dog deserves — built to last,
  beautiful enough to leave out."* Quality + durability + design.
- **Creative ideas:** UGC "6 months later, still perfect"; harness fit demo;
  before/after (ratty gear → Loomipaw); 5-star review montage.

## Segment 2 — Premium pet buyers (highest LTV)

- **Pain points:** mass-market brands feel generic; want craftsmanship,
  materials, and ethics that match how they shop for themselves.
- **Buying triggers:** brand story, materials (merino, cashmere, full-grain
  leather), sustainability, exclusivity/new drops.
- **Best products:** Cloud Orthopedic Lounger ($189), Cashmere-Blend Throw ($84),
  Heritage Leather Collar ($52), Storm Weatherproof Coat ($92).
- **Advertising angle:** *"The Apple/Aesop of pet."* Understated luxury,
  OEKO-TEX®, carbon-neutral, considered design.
- **Creative ideas:** slow macro of materials/stitching; founder philosophy;
  "designed for the bond" lifestyle film; unboxing ASMR.

## Segment 3 — Gift buyers

- **Pain points:** want something that looks premium and arrives on time;
  unsure of size; fear of a "cheap-looking" gift.
- **Buying triggers:** birthdays, holidays, new-puppy in a friend's family,
  "gift for the person who has everything (and a dog)."
- **Best products:** **bundles** (Identity Set ~$99, Matching Moment ~$88),
  Explorer Brass ID Tag ($34), Duo Matching Scarf Set ($68), gift-friendly
  accessories.
- **Advertising angle:** *"The dog-lover gift that doesn't look like a dog gift."*
  Giftability + easy returns + fast shipping.
- **Creative ideas:** gift-guide carousel; "reactions" to opening; size-safe
  message ("free 30-day exchanges"); matching human+pet as a couple/family gift.

## Segment 4 — New pet parents

- **Pain points:** overwhelmed, don't know what to buy, want to "do it right,"
  budget-aware but quality-seeking.
- **Buying triggers:** just got a puppy/rescue, starter-kit searches, vet/breeder
  recommendations, nesting phase.
- **Best products:** starter bundles — **Complete Walk Kit** (Harness + Lead +
  ID Tag ~$128), collar + tag, first bed.
- **Advertising angle:** *"Everything your new best friend needs, done right the
  first time."* Curated starter kits, guidance, guarantee.
- **Creative ideas:** "new puppy checklist" explainer; how-to-measure guide;
  founder "what I wish I'd bought first"; bundle walkthrough.

## Segment 5 — Senior / special-needs dog owners

- **Pain points:** joint pain, mobility, pressure sores, dogs that "destroy"
  cheap beds; want genuine comfort and support.
- **Buying triggers:** vet advice, visible aging/limping, cold weather (achy
  joints), replacing a failed bed.
- **Best products:** Cloud Orthopedic Lounger ($189), Cashmere-Blend Throw ($84),
  Aspen Merino Knit ($78, warmth), soft Heritage Collar.
- **Advertising angle:** *"Comfort that loves them back."* Orthopedic support,
  vet-informed, easy on aging joints — emotional + functional.
- **Creative ideas:** senior-dog testimonial ("she finally sleeps through the
  night"); orthopedic foam demo; cozy winter-warmth story; comfort-test footage.

## Acquisition priorities (first 90 days)

1. **Everyday + Premium** on Meta/TikTok prospecting — largest, most scalable.
2. **New pet parents + Senior** via SEO how-to content (high intent, low CAC).
3. **Gift buyers** seasonally (holiday spikes) with bundle offers.
4. Retarget all segments; convert with the email flows in
   `EMAIL_MARKETING_STRATEGY.md`.

**North star:** blended CAC < 40–50% of AOV, with LTV expanded by post-purchase
email + repeat/bundle purchasing. (Targets, not guarantees.)
