# Loomipaw — Content & Creative System

A repeatable short-form video engine for organic (IG Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
and paid (Meta/TikTok ads). Creative is ~70% of paid performance, so this is the
highest-leverage marketing asset.

**Format standard:** 9:16, 15–30s, hook in first 1.5s, captions always on,
one clear idea + one CTA. Shoot in batches; repurpose winners across organic
and paid. Segments referenced from `CUSTOMER_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md`.

## Production workflow

1. **Batch shoot** 8–12 clips/session against this list (props: 2–3 hero SKUs + a dog).
2. **Edit to spec:** hook first, captions, trending-but-brand-safe audio, 15–25s.
3. **Label** each asset `bucket-idea#-variant` for test tracking.
4. **Post organic first** → the top performers become paid ads (Spark/whitelisted).
5. **Refresh weekly:** 5–10 new variants to fight fatigue (frequency > 2.5–3).
6. **Track** thumbstop (3-sec view), hold (avg watch), CTR, CPA per asset in
   `PROFIT_METRICS_PLAN.md`. Double down on hook patterns that win.

**Priority to shoot first:** #1, #5, #9, #16, #23, #30, #38 — the highest-intent,
most broadly relevant hooks across core segments.
