# Loomipaw — First 100 Customers Plan

Getting the first 100 real customers is about **proof, not scale**: validate the
funnel, collect honest conversion + margin data, and gather reviews/UGC that
make everything after cheaper. Prioritize low-cost, high-trust channels first;
treat paid as measured testing, not a growth lever yet (`AD_SCALING_RULEBOOK.md`).

**Goal:** 100 paying customers with clean, reconciled data (`purchase` verified
across GA4/Meta/TikTok) and ≥ 25 reviews.

**Assumptions (estimates, not guarantees):** AOV $85–120 · site CVR 1.5–3% ·
premium pet DTC. Adjust to your real numbers as they land.

---

## Channel plan

### 1. Organic social (IG / TikTok / Pinterest)
- **Daily actions:** 1–2 posts/day from `CONTENT_CREATIVE_SYSTEM.md` (start with
  #1, #5, #9, #16, #23, #30, #38); engage 20–30 dog accounts; reply to every
  comment/DM.
- **Expected metrics:** follower growth; 3-sec view rate ≥ 25%; saves/shares as
  the leading indicator; profile → site clicks.
- **Cost:** ~$0 (time).
- **Success criteria:** ≥ 1 post breaking out (10k+ views) in week 1–2; steady
  profile-link traffic converting at site CVR.

### 2. Short-form video (the engine)
- **Daily actions:** batch-shoot weekly (8–12 clips); post the best daily; repost
  winners across all three platforms.
- **Expected metrics:** watch-through + shares; the winners become paid ads later.
- **Cost:** ~$0–low (props/product).
- **Success criteria:** identify 2–3 repeatable hook formats that hold attention.

### 3. Influencers / creators (gifting first)
- **Daily actions:** outreach to 10–15 micro creators/day (5k–50k, dog niche);
  offer free product + affiliate code; prioritize gifting over cash.
- **Expected metrics:** reply rate 10–20%; content pieces live; referral orders
  via codes.
- **Cost:** product COGS + occasional small fee.
- **Success criteria:** 5–10 creators posting in month 1; ≥ 1 with real sell-through
  → whitelist for paid.

### 4. Communities (Reddit, FB groups, Discords, local)
- **Daily actions:** participate genuinely in dog-owner communities; help first,
  share only where allowed/relevant; local dog parks/events for real-world seeding.
- **Expected metrics:** referral traffic; qualitative objections/questions
  (feed `CUSTOMER_FEEDBACK_SYSTEM.md`).
- **Cost:** ~$0.
- **Success criteria:** steady trickle of high-intent visitors; recurring
  questions answered on-site (FAQ/size guide).

### 5. Paid testing (measured, not scaling)
- **Daily actions:** only **after** tracking verified; $50–100/day cold tests per
  `PAID_AD_TESTING_PLAN.md`; retarget site visitors + cart abandoners.
- **Expected metrics:** CPA, ROAS, CTR, thumbstop per ad.
- **Cost:** ~$1,500–3,000 across the push (front-loaded testing).
- **Success criteria:** ≥ 1 profitable ad set at CPA ≤ 40–50% of AOV; **do not
  scale** until the gate clears.

## Also-on (compounding, low effort)
- **Email:** welcome (10% off) + abandoned-cart flows live from day 1 — recovers
  a chunk of the first cohort (`EMAIL_MARKETING_STRATEGY.md`).
- **Warm network:** friends/family/waitlist launch note.
- **SEO:** publish the first buying guides (`SEO_GROWTH_PLAN.md`) — slow burn,
  starts compounding for customers 100→1000.

## Sequenced path to 100 (illustrative)

| Phase | Focus | Rough contribution |
|-------|-------|--------------------|
| Days 1–7 | Organic + video + warm list + gifting outreach | first 10–25 |
| Days 8–21 | Creators posting + paid cold tests + retargeting/email recovery | next ~40 |
| Days 22–30+ | Double down on what converts (best channel × best creative) | to 100 |

Numbers depend entirely on content resonance and traffic quality — the plan is
the **sequence and discipline**, not a promise of specific counts.

---

## What "success" really means at 100 customers

Not vanity volume — **validated economics + assets**:
- [ ] `purchase` tracking reconciles with real orders (GA4/Meta/TikTok).
- [ ] Real CVR, AOV, and channel CPAs measured (`CONVERSION_OPTIMIZATION_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`).
- [ ] Contribution margin **after CAC** known and (ideally) positive.
- [ ] ≥ 25 reviews + a library of UGC.
- [ ] ≥ 1 profitable, repeatable acquisition channel identified.
- [ ] Top objections + refund reasons catalogued (`CUSTOMER_FEEDBACK_SYSTEM.md`).

Hit those and you have the data to decide — confidently — what to scale.
